# Macinity changelog

## English

### Macinity 3.1.0 — 2026-10-16

Action Center adds favorites and existing-state summaries. Smart Diagnostics adds explicit, timestamped symptom analysis and comparable-observation deltas. Work Profiles extends compatible workspace presets with reviewed audio/Keep Awake steps and per-step results. See [scope and verification](docs/product/ACTION-WORKFLOWS.md). Cobalt Link branding, the utility suite, battery history and separate Disk/Network navigation are included.

### Orven 3.0.0

Orven is the new product name and ring identity for Toolbox. The native interface adds live monitoring, hardware profiles and available mapped sensors, Sleep Inspector, Wi-Fi/LAN observations and explicit diagnostics, optional health history/baselines, sensor recording/exports, smart alerts, session connection changes, app dossiers, growth/workspace tools and local reports. Existing installer evidence, reviewed storage actions and recovery remain.

### Compatibility and trust

Bundle ID, executable/module identifiers and Application Support/Toolbox remain compatible. English, Vietnamese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese are packaged. The explicitly authorized distribution is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. No Homebrew, firewall, fan control, automatic CPU cap/suspension, exclusive snapshot-byte accounting or calibrated CPU-Z/HWMonitor parity is claimed.

### Validation and known limits

Tests, source/locale checks, universal packaging and exact release artifact verification are recorded in [readiness](docs/production/RELEASE-READINESS.md). Current foreground profiling, physical Intel/macOS 13/Ethernet, full VoiceOver, Japanese critical-copy review and native notification delivery remain unverified. Earlier resource deviations are not a current-package pass. Prior Toolbox releases remain in [GitHub release history](https://github.com/thangldw/toolbox/releases).
