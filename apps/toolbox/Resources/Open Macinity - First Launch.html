Open Macinity for the first time

Macinity 3.0.0 is not Apple-notarized. macOS therefore requires one manual approval. Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes.

  1. Drag Macinity to the Applications shortcut in this window.
  2. Open Macinity from Applications once. macOS will block this first attempt.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Find the Macinity message, click Open Anyway, then authenticate.
  5. Confirm Open. Later launches should open normally.

Verify the downloaded DMG before installation:

shasum -a 256 -c Macinity-3.0.0.dmg.sha256

Mở Macinity lần đầu

Macinity 3.0.0 chưa được Apple notarize. Kéo Macinity vào Applications, thử mở một lần, sau đó vào System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway và xác thực. Không tắt Gatekeeper hoặc xóa quarantine attribute.
