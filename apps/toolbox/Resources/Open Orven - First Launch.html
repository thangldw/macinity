Open Orven for the first time

Orven 3.0.0 is not Apple-notarized. macOS therefore requires one manual approval. Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes.

  1. Drag Orven to the Applications shortcut in this window.
  2. Open Orven from Applications once. macOS will block this first attempt.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Find the Orven message, click Open Anyway, then authenticate.
  5. Confirm Open. Later launches should open normally.

Verify the downloaded DMG before installation:

shasum -a 256 -c Orven-3.0.0.dmg.sha256

Mở Orven lần đầu

Orven 3.0.0 chưa được Apple notarize. Kéo Orven vào Applications, thử mở một lần, sau đó vào System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway và xác thực. Không tắt Gatekeeper hoặc xóa quarantine attribute.
