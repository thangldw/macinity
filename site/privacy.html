Macinity · Privacy

Your data stays under your control.

No telemetry or automatic upload. Explicit probes contact chosen destinations.

Local state and optional history

Management data remains in the existing local app-data directory for compatibility. Optional health history stores system measurements only, with 24-hour or seven-day retention and a 24 MiB limit. Sensor recordings, alerts and connection timelines are bounded session data. Expired on-disk records are removed during a later successful write, not while the app is stopped.

Network and permissions

DNS, ICMP and TCP probes expose ordinary connection metadata to the chosen destination or resolver. Nearby Wi-Fi scans are explicit OS operations; identities may be redacted. Optional Location, Local Network, filesystem and notification permissions expand coverage, never authorize deletion. Update checks contact GitHub.

Review before sharing

Reports are previewed and saved locally. Diagnostic reports may contain app/process names, paths and endpoints; replacing a home prefix is not complete anonymization. Review every report before sharing. There is no automatic upload.
